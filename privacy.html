Home app: privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Home app is a private household organiser, used only by the family that runs it. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses in Google

Only the Google Calendar permission calendar.app.created: it creates one calendar of its own, "Home", and adds, changes and removes events on that calendar. It cannot read or change any other calendar, and it has no access to email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

What it stores

The household's to-do items stay on the family's own computer. The sign-in key Google gives the app is kept in that computer's protected credential store. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or for training AI models.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access), and can delete the "Home" calendar in Google Calendar.

Contact

Use the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
